# Public beta: owner walkthroughs

Goal: find the point where an owner cannot decide what to do next. A completed
website task is separate from a successful physical print. No recruited-owner
or printer-validation results are claimed yet.

Invite three to five owners with different experience levels. Ask them to use
their normal phone or computer next to the printer. Let them talk through what
they expect; avoid telling them which answer to choose. Use only tasks relevant
to the printer's current state and the owner's comfort.

1. **New owner:** “You've assembled the U1. Find the next preparation check.”
2. **Filament:** “Your spool has no RFID label. Find how to load it and tell us
   what would show you that loading finished.”
3. **Troubleshooting:** “Find a guide for a symptom or a complete error code you
   actually encountered. Explain its stop condition.”
4. **Next project:** “Find one useful print for your setup. Identify its creator,
   what else is needed, and what the site has not verified.”
5. **Planner:** “Build a plan for your actual budget. Explain a reason chip and
   one deferred or excluded option. Share the link and restore it.”

Record task completion, wrong turns, requests for help and missing information.
Use the owner's words rather than inferring a diagnosis. Record whether they
only browsed, followed along on a U1 or completed a physical print. Firmware
version may be relevant; serial numbers, IP addresses and account credentials
are not needed.

Use the public Owner walkthrough feedback issue template, or ask the owner for
permission before publishing their notes or photographs. Fix blockers before
adding new catalog sections. Retest the failed task after a repair. Keep actual
observations and measured outcomes separate from proposed improvements.

Release focus: first-print guidance, reliable navigation, understandable plan
reasons and truthful source status. No new feature categories during this beta.
