What to budget

Before you start

  • Complete stock burn-in
  • Mainboard and PSU cooling review
  • Keep stock firmware for at least 7 days and finish clean stock tests first.
  • Requires soldering skills and a complete installation guide.

Read before changing anything

Warranty risk: Gray. Review the current terms and the creator's guide.

  • Requires a complete electronics-cooling guide, specified fans and hardware. Do not open or modify a mains PSU from this summary; use qualified help. A driver-temperature report is not processor-cooling certification.
  • Optional after clean stock checks. Current model instructions and physical fit have not been verified.
  • The listed time is our planning allowance for review/fitting, excluding printing; it is not a creator-tested print duration.
