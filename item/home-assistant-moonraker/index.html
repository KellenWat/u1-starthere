Will this work for my setup?

Printer / firmware
Needs reachable Moonraker and compatible Home Assistant/HACS. The linked U1 monitoring guide follows an extended-firmware setup.
Accounts and services
An existing Home Assistant installation and HACS setup are needed; authentication depends on your Moonraker configuration.
Extra hardware
A separate Home Assistant host and network access to Moonraker.
