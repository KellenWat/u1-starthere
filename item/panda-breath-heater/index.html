Before you start

  • Complete stock burn-in
  • Mainboard and PSU cooling review
  • Keep stock firmware for at least 7 days and finish clean stock tests first.
  • Needs an enclosure. Review a suitable route: Official U1 Top Cover, DIY IKEA SAMLA lid, BIQU PopCap cover.
  • Needs electronics cooling for chamber heating. Review a suitable route: Mainboard and PSU cooling review.

Read before changing anything

Warranty risk: Void risk. Review the current terms and the creator's guide.

  • Do not operate without processor/mainboard cooling and a suitable enclosure.
  • Active chamber heating can cause overheating and damage; follow the U1 integration guide and official support advice.
  • A product target temperature does not establish a safe operating temperature for the printer.
