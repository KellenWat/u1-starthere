Phaetus Liber high-flow hotends

A high-flow hotend set targets large parts with long extrusion paths. Check material restrictions and calibrate the matching heads after replacement.

What to budget

Price recorded Oct 26, 2026. Check the seller before ordering.

Before you start

  • Complete stock burn-in
  • Check the matching-hotend requirement for all heads used in the job.
  • Unsuitable material route: Tpu, Wood, Cf gf.

Read before changing anything

  • Checklist and product page advise against TPU, wood and CF/GF in this high-flow route.
  • Keep all heads used in the job matched; review the current hotend settings guide.
