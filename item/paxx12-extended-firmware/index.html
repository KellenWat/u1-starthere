Firmware / Firmware decision

Paxx12 Extended Firmware

Adds community integrations and browser controls to the U1. Read the installation and stock-recovery instructions before changing firmware.

Cost
Free
Estimated time
1 hr 30 min
Difficulty
Hands-on 3 of 5
Reversible
Usually, yes

What to budget

No purchase price; printed parts still consume your filament and power.

Before you start

  • Complete stock burn-in
  • Keep stock firmware for at least 3 days and finish clean stock tests first.

Read before changing anything

Warranty risk: Gray. Review the current terms and the creator's guide.

  • Wait until stock burn-in is clean; the checklist schedules installation on Days 4–5.
  • Damage attributable to custom firmware may not be covered; review the current terms.

Optional next steps

These are separate choices, not required purchases.

  • Enable Spoolman integration
  • Enable print notifications
  • Set up the WebRTC camera
  • Review Tailscale remote access
  • HelixScreen touchscreen interface
  • OctoEverywhere cloud access
  • Remote touchscreen view
  • Choose Fluidd or Mainsail
